V48 - EDITORIAL CONTROL CENTER
================================

1. Sao luu website va database tren hosting.
2. Giai nen file ZIP tren may tinh.
3. Upload cac thu muc classes, includes, modules, templates len thu muc goc
   cua website bang FileZilla. Chon ghi de (overwrite) khi duoc hoi.
4. Khong upload thu muc templates_c trong goi cu hoac xoa code nguon cu.
5. Mo phpMyAdmin, chon database cua website va import:
   database/20260921_v48_editorial_control_center.sql
6. Dang nhap Admin va mo:
   /admin.php?op=editorial
7. Chon Cover Story, Featured, Trending Override va Video noi bat, sau do luu.
8. Kiem tra trang chu, trang Video va mot trang chi tiet bai viet.

Rollback database (chi dung khi can go bo V48):
database/20260921_v48_editorial_control_center_rollback.sql

Luu y: rollback se xoa cau hinh noi dung noi bat va lich su luot doc theo ngay.
